## Direction contract

THESIS: "The Academy Register." The portal reads as EuroAfrica's institutional registry of learners and programmes — not a SaaS dashboard and not a brochure. It refuses the grey-screen-with-one-accent default and the icon-card landing: identity is carried by ruled ledger structure, a serif institutional voice, and a stamped folio on every real record.

OWN-WORLD: Committed institutional navy shell (deep EuroAfrica blue) + warm ivory reading surfaces + ink text + registry-blue actions + a restrained gold used only as a seal/stamp. Serif display (self-hosted book face) for titles and the Academy wordmark; workhorse sans for body/controls. Hairline rules tile dense data; folio numbers in ledger type; one spaced small-caps register line acts as each surface's own heading label.

STORY: A member lands, recognises the authentic mark + "Academy", signs in (EuroAfrica SSO primary), and reaches their role workspace. Each enrolment, order, session and result carries its real reference as a stamped folio — proof that this is a registry of record, not a demo.
